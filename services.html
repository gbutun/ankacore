Cloud Strategy, Architecture, and Landing Zones

Design Azure foundations that balance governance, cost control, security, and delivery speed. This includes cloud adoption planning, enterprise architecture decisions, landing zones, subscription strategy, identity, networking, and operating model guidance.

Build or improve internal platform capabilities with delivery pipelines, GitOps or CI/CD patterns, Kubernetes operating models, and platform guardrails. The focus is on making engineering teams faster, safer, and easier to scale.

Strengthen security posture across cloud platforms and engineering workflows with practical architecture guidance. Engagements can cover identity, access, Zero-Trust architecture, pipeline hardening, platform risk reviews, and secure operating patterns for regulated sectors.

Automation, IaC, and AI-Assisted Delivery Improvement

Improve consistency and delivery speed with Terraform, Ansible, reusable templates, automation standards, and modern AI-assisted engineering workflows. The objective is better throughput without compromising control or auditability.

  • Practical AI enablement inside engineering and operations workflows
